# PB-34 Make merging worth it

Owner: Emir · Milestone: M6 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-34-merge-rework` · From: the 2026-11-21 review

## Why
Merging is a pillar (GDD §6) but today it makes the army weaker. With the balance bots, "Merge all" before the
battle drops wins from 75% to 14% on 2-3, 70% to 18% on 2-6 and 47% to 9% on 2-10. Three T1 units are 3.0x the
stats of one; the T2 they make is 1.8x, and a T3 (nine T1) is 3.2x. With 16 slots and 10-21 recruits, space never
runs out, so merging is never right. Yet the 1-2 tutorial says "Try Merge all" and quests pay for merging.

## Today
- Multipliers in `Data/ArmyRules.asset` (`ArmyRules`), merge in `Logic/Army/MergeRules.cs`, `Roster.MergeAll()`.
- `Tools/Balance` bots never merge (`Logic/Flow/BalanceRunner.cs`), so the problem was invisible.
- This is Alican's area (rules, data, balance): he reviews the branch before the merge into `main`.

## Build
1. A merge strategy in the balance runner and tool, so every table shows no-merge and merge-all.
2. New multipliers so one T2 beats the three T1 it costs (target about 3.5x, T3 about 11x); check whether slots
   ever matter, and if not consider fewer slots.
3. Re-run the balance table; stage enemies re-tuned so win rates with merging land where the no-merge rates were.
4. Tests: a merged unit is stronger than its parts; "Merge all" never lowers the win rate of a stage much.
5. Decision in DECISIONS.md with the before/after table.

## Not in this card
New tiers, new merge rules (cross-class), art.
