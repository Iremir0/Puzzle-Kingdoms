# PB-27 Async PvP: defence armies

Owner: — · Milestone: M9 · Size: L · Branch: `feature/PB-27-async-pvp` ·
Wave: 4 (D-045) · Needs: backend decision, PB-23

## Why
Playing against other people keeps players for months. Real-time PvP is too heavy
for two developers; asynchronous PvP is not: a player saves a defence formation, others
attack it. Because the battle is deterministic, the server only stores formations and
can re-run any battle to check a result.

## Today
- Battles replay exactly from armies + rules + Rally ticks (ARCHITECTURE, "Fixed-tick combat").
- Formations are 4×4 grids ([Formation](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Army/Formation.cs)).
- No backend, no accounts.

## Build
1. **Backend decision first** (Firebase or Unity Gaming Services; free tiers), recorded
   in DECISIONS.md; Alican opens the account.
2. **Logic:** a defence record (formation, tiers, commander, kingdom levels), the attack
   battle built from it, trophy change. Tests, including that a re-run gives the same result.
3. **Attack flow:** a short puzzle (fewer moves) to build the attacking army, War Camp,
   battle against the stored defence.
4. **Server:** store defences, match by trophies, verify submitted results by re-running.
5. **Views:** Arena screen (my defence, opponents, log of attacks on me), weekly league.
