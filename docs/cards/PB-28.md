# PB-28 Battle replays and sharing

Owner: — · Milestone: M9 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-28-replays` ·
Wave: 4 (D-045) · Needs: —

## Why
A great Rally at the last second is worth showing. A replay costs almost nothing to
store (the battle is rebuilt from its inputs), and a short clip shared by players is
free advertising.

## Today
- A battle is reproducible from `(armies, rules, InputLog)` (ARCHITECTURE);
  [MoveReplay](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Puzzle/MoveReplay.cs) does the same for puzzle moves.
- The 3D battle draws into a texture ([BattleWorld3D](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Views/Battle3D/BattleWorld3D.cs)).

## Build
1. **Logic:** `BattleReplay` (seed, both armies, rules id, Rally ticks) with a small text
   format and a version number; load and re-simulate. Tests: saved → loaded → same events.
2. **Profile:** keep the last N replays and the ones the player stars.
3. **Views:** replay player with a timeline (Rally and deaths marked), play / pause /
   2×, seek by re-simulating up to a tick.
4. **Share:** record a short video of the replay (native share sheet on Android); pick a
   plugin or Unity's recorder path in the card, add it to CREDITS.md first.
