# PB-38 Rewards worth chasing

Owner: Emir · Milestone: M6 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-38-economy-feel` · From: the 2026-11-21 review

## Why
Gold buys only +3% buildings (19,690 gold to max, about 90 replays). Replays pay full gold, so farming is the loop.
One star chest in the whole game (every 30 of 39 stars). The results screen draws stars as `* * -` text and there is
no haptic feedback anywhere.

## Build
1. First-clear bonus, replays pay less (closes D-022): numbers in `ProgressionRules`.
2. Star chest every 12 stars.
3. Results screen: real star shapes that pop in one by one.
4. Light haptics on merge, rally and victory, with a Settings switch.
5. "Out of moves: watch an ad for +5 moves" when a puzzle ends short of its goal (rewarded, once per stage).
