# PB-37 Unlock systems step by step

Owner: Emir · Milestone: M6 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-37-gradual-unlocks` · From: the 2026-11-21 review

## Why
All five tabs, the Daily Challenge and the Expedition are open on the first launch, and the two modes use
Chapter 2 enemies the player has not met. Blast, Prism and combos are never taught. Research: show the value in the
first 2-3 minutes, one new thing per stage.

## Build
1. An unlock rule in Logic (feature → stage that opens it): Kingdom and Quests after 1-2, Commanders, Shop and
   Daily after 1-3, Expedition after 2-3.
2. Locked tabs and buttons show what opens them; the first time a feature opens, one intro card says what it is.
3. Stage 1-4 "The Quarry" joins the campaign as the Blast and Prism lesson (tutorial prompts on the stage).

## Done when
- [x] Unlock rule with tests; tabs and modes locked until their stage
- [x] Intro card once per feature; 1-4 in the campaign teaching specials
- [x] Turkish texts added
