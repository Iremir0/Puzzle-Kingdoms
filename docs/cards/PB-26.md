# PB-26 Expedition mode (roguelite)

Owner: — · Milestone: M8 · Size: L · Branch: `feature/PB-26-expedition` ·
Wave: 3 (D-045) · Needs: PB-6 "go"

## Why
A longer session for players who want more than one stage: five battles in a row,
the army and its wounds carry over, and between battles the player picks one of three
rewards (GDD §10, post-MVP item 4).

## Today
- A run is one stage: [StageRun](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Flow/StageRun.cs)
  goes Puzzle → War Camp → Battle → reward, and the army is thrown away after.
- Survivors and their HP are known at the end of a battle ([BattleResult](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Combat/BattleResult.cs)).

## Build
1. **Logic:** `Expedition` — seed, five steps (last one a boss), the army carried over
   with HP, a short puzzle before each battle (fewer moves), reward picks between
   battles (class bonus, extra Rally, promote a unit), end on a loss. Tests: carry-over,
   rewards apply, same seed gives the same expedition.
2. **Data:** expedition rules and the reward pool on an asset; enemy pool per step.
3. **Profile:** best depth, a running expedition saved between sessions.
4. **Views:** path screen, reward pick, the army panel with HP bars; reuse puzzle,
   War Camp and battle screens.
