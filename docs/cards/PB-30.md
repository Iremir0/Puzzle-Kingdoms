# PB-30 Economy and revenue

Owner: — · Milestone: M8 · Size: L · Branch: `feature/PB-30-economy` ·
Wave: 3 (D-045) · Needs: decision on the business model, PB-22, PB-29 (consent)

## Why
The game has to pay for itself without breaking pillar 2: power is never sold. Rewarded
ads the player chooses, an ad-free pack and a season pass of mostly cosmetic rewards are
the plan; the exact model (free with ads and purchases, or paid once) is the owner's call.

## Today
- One currency (gold), earned per stage ([StageReward](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Progression/StageReward.cs)).
- No ads, no purchases, no shop.

## Build
1. **Business model decision** recorded in DECISIONS.md (free + ads + purchases, or paid).
2. **Logic:** wallet (gold + crowns), offers, season pass track (free and premium rows),
   rewarded ad grants (+5 moves once per stage, extra Rally once per battle, gold with a
   daily cap). Tests: caps, no double grants, pass progress.
3. **Ads:** rewarded ads (and interstitials if the model has them) behind an `IAds`
   interface; personalised ads only with consent (PB-29). Add the SDK to CREDITS.md.
4. **Purchases:** Unity IAP (ad-free pack, crowns, pass), receipt check, restore purchases.
5. **Views:** Shop screen, "out of moves" offer, pass screen.
6. Store setup: products in Google Play Console (owner).
