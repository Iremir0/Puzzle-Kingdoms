# PB-18 Chapter 2: Goblin Fort

Owner: Alican · Milestone: M7 · Size: L · Branch: `feature/PB-18-chapter-2` ·
Wave: 2 (D-045) · Needs: PB-6 "go", PB-19 (stage tool)

## Why
Three stages are a demo, not a game. Chapter 2 doubles-plus the content and is the
first place a player meets enemies that change how they recruit: a shaman that must
die first, a wolf rider that dives the back row. Each stage teaches one new idea, as
1-1 to 1-3 do today.

## Build
1. **Design the chapter** (in GDD §11): 10–12 stages, one lesson each, the last one
   a boss stage (Chief + guards). Fill the table: name, enemies, formation, stones,
   multipliers, lesson.
2. **Four new enemy behaviours in Logic**, each with tests:
   - Shield goblin: takes less damage from ranged attacks while it is in front.
   - Shaman: heals the most hurt ally in range every N ticks.
   - Wolf rider: fast; targets the enemy back row first.
   - Bomber: area damage around its target.
3. **Data:** a `UnitDefinition` per enemy, stage assets `Stage_2_1 ... Stage_2_12` made
   with the PB-19 tool, `GameContent` campaign list extended, chapter field on the stage.
4. **Balance:** every stage through `Tools/Balance` (win rate per stage rising, like D-033).
5. **Art:** four Meshy models (prompts in ART.md), rigged and imported by `MeshyRigImport`.
6. **Map:** a second chapter map (fort look), chapter switch on the stage map.
