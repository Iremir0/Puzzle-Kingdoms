# PB-41 Chapter 3 enemies

Owner: Emir · Milestone: M8 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-41-chapter-3-enemies` · From: the 2026-11-21 review (variety)

## Why
Chapter 2 taught four enemy behaviours. A new chapter needs new problems to solve, not only bigger numbers.

## Build
Three behaviours as data on `UnitDef`, each with one lesson:
1. **Bog Troll**: regenerates a share of its max HP every second. Lesson: put your damage together (merge, Mages, Rally).
2. **Goblin Berserker**: attacks twice as fast below half HP. Lesson: finish what you start; the Defender holds it.
3. **Marsh Sapper**: explodes when it dies and hurts the units near it. Lesson: kill it from range.
4. **Troll King** (boss): regenerates and goes berserk.

The battle views show the new events (regen numbers, an "enraged" mark, the explosion). The models stand in with
Chapter 1 silhouettes until the Meshy models exist (ART.md).
