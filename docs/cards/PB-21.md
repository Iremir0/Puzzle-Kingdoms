# PB-21 New classes: Defender and Support

Owner: — · Milestone: M7 · Size: L · Branch: `feature/PB-21-defender-support` ·
Wave: 2 (D-045) · Needs: PB-6 "go"

## Why
Three classes give few army shapes. A tank that holds the front and a healer that
keeps it alive add real formation choices, and their gems change the board (GDD §10,
post-MVP item 2).

## Today
- Classes Warrior / Archer / Mage, one gem type each plus the Banner (Rally) gem
  ([PuzzleRules](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Puzzle/PuzzleRules.cs),
  [RecruitmentMeter](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Puzzle/RecruitmentMeter.cs)).
- Combat roles are in [BattleSimulator](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Combat/BattleSimulator.cs)
  and [Targeting](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Combat/Targeting.cs).

## Build
1. **Design** in GDD §7: Defender (high HP and armour, draws attacks: enemies in front of
   it target it first), Support (heals the most hurt ally in range, low damage). Gems:
   Defender a blue shield, Support a pink cross (colour check with `ContrastTests`).
2. **A stage chooses its gem types** (max 5 on the board): e.g. Warrior, Archer, Defender,
   Support + Banner. Default = today's set. Tests that the board only uses those types.
3. **Logic** for taunt and heal, tier scaling and merge as today. Tests.
4. **Data:** two `UnitDefinition` assets, `UnitLook`, gem order in `GameContent`.
5. **Art:** two Meshy heroes (ART.md), rigs, a heal effect and a shield effect in 3D.
6. **Unlock:** both classes are chapter rewards (end of Chapter 1 / middle of Chapter 2).
7. Balance pass with `Tools/Balance`.
