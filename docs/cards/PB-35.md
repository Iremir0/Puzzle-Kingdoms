# PB-35 Battle tempo and fair stages

Owner: Emir · Milestone: M6 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-35-tempo-fair-stages` · From: the 2026-11-21 review

## Why
Chapter 2 wins take 38-49 s (target 20-40 s, GDD §2) and a battle that hits 60 s is a defeat. Some stages are not
fair: 2-2's ice goal fails about 15% of the time whatever the player does, 2-8 is 59% without Rally and 100% with
it, 2-9 and 2-10 drop to 63% and 47% for a good bot. A defeat does not say why. Alican reviews (his area).

## Build
1. Faster battles: damage up or HP down globally (`Data/BattleRules.asset`) until Chapter 2 wins take 25-35 s.
2. Time-out: when time runs out the side with more HP left (in %) wins, instead of an automatic defeat.
3. 2-2: make the ice goal always reachable; prove it with the bots (goal met in every run).
4. 2-8: less dependent on Rally (target: 75-85% without, still higher with).
5. Smooth 2-9 and 2-10 to 65-75% for the greedy bot.
6. Defeat hint on the results screen: one line from the battle result (outnumbered, enemy too strong, time ran out).
