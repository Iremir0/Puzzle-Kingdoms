# PB-39 Expedition perk draft

Owner: Emir · Milestone: M8 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-39-expedition-perks` · From: the 2026-11-21 review

## Why
The Expedition repeats five battles with three classes. A pick-one-of-three perk after each battle (the Archero
pattern) gives every run a different shape at little cost.

## Build
1. Perks as data: +HP for a class, +damage for a class, faster rally charge, heal the wounded, etc.
2. After each won battle, three seeded perks; the pick is saved in `ExpeditionSave` and applied in battle.
3. Defender and Support join the Expedition pool once unlocked.

- [ ] Pick screen in the Expedition flow
