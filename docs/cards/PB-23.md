# PB-23 Commander heroes

Owner: — · Milestone: M7 · Size: L · Branch: `feature/PB-23-commanders` ·
Wave: 2 (D-045) · Needs: PB-6 "go", PB-22 (tab bar)

## Why
Rally is our signature move, and today it is the same in every battle. A commander
leads the army and changes what Rally does, so the same stage plays differently with
a different commander. Commanders are earned by playing (stage rewards), never from
a loot box: losses must stay the player's own decisions (GDD pillar 2).

## Today
- One Rally kind: attack speed and damage up for a few seconds
  ([Rally](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Combat/Rally.cs), GDD §9),
  charges earned with Banner gems.
- Battle input is a log of Rally ticks, so battles replay exactly (ARCHITECTURE).

## Build
1. **Design** (GDD §9 and §10): three commanders, each a Rally variant —
   *Shield Wall* (front row takes less damage), *Arrow Rain* (damage to the enemy back
   row over a few seconds), *Fireball* (area damage on the densest enemy group).
   Unlock stages.
2. **Logic:** `RallyKind` on the battle input; each variant as its own small class with
   tests; the commander does not fight (keeps the 4×4 grid as it is).
3. **Data:** `CommanderDefinition` assets (name, Rally kind, numbers, unlock stage, look).
4. **Profile:** unlocked commanders and the chosen one (save version bump, migration).
5. **Views:** Commanders screen (pick one), commander shown in the War Camp and the
   battle HUD, a VFX per Rally kind in 3D.
6. **Art:** three Meshy commander models.
7. Balance: each stage winnable with each commander (`Tools/Balance`).
