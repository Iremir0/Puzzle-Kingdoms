# PB-24 Daily quests, achievements, star chests

Owner: — · Milestone: M8 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-24-quests` ·

## Why
A small daily reason to open the game: three or four short goals a day, and stars
that keep paying after a stage is finished.

## Today
- The run already reports what happened in an attempt (merges, Rally used, units
  recruited per class): [StageAttempt](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Flow/StageAttempt.cs) (D-037).
- Stars and gold in the profile.

## Build
1. **Logic:** `QuestBoard` — quest kinds (win N stages, make N Blasts, use Rally N times,
   reach T3, ...), progress fed from `StageAttempt`, daily reset by date (the date is
   passed in; Logic has no clock), rewards. Achievements as one-time quests. Star chest:
   every 30 stars, a reward. Tests, including the day change.
2. **Data:** quest pool and rewards on an asset.
3. **Profile:** quest state, claimed achievements, chest progress (save version bump).
4. **Views:** Quests screen (daily / achievements tabs), a badge on the tab bar when
   something can be claimed.
