# PB-40 Stage generator and tuning bot

Owner: Emir · Milestone: M8 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-40-stage-generator` · From: the 2026-11-21 review (content)

## Why
The game has 14 stages, about an hour of play. Hand-making and hand-tuning each stage takes most of a day. Puzzle
games of this kind reach launch with many more stages by generating candidates and keeping the ones a bot finds fair.
The balance bots already play a stage; what is missing is a way to make candidates and to tune them to a target.

## Build
1. `StageGenerator` (Logic): from a blueprint (enemy pool, army size, tiers, goal, obstacles, board seed) makes a
   seeded `StageDef`. Same blueprint and seed, same stage.
2. `StageTuner` (Logic, the "solver"): plays a stage with the greedy + Merge-all bot and searches the enemy HP
   multiplier until the win rate lands in a target band; reports win rate, win time and goal misses.
3. `Tools/Balance --tune`: tunes the campaign stages (or `--only`) to a band and prints the multiplier to write.

## Done when
- [ ] Generator and tuner in Logic with tests (seeded, reaches the band, gives up cleanly when it cannot)
- [ ] Balance tool can tune a stage from the terminal
