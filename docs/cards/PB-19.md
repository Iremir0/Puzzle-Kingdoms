# PB-19 Stage tool and stage goals

Owner: Alican · Milestone: M7 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-19-stage-tool` ·
Wave: 1 (D-045) · Needs: —

## Why
The soft launch needs about 40 stages; today a stage is made by hand in the
Inspector and its difficulty is checked by running `Tools/Balance` from a terminal.
A tool that paints the board and shows the win rate on one screen makes a stage in
minutes. Stage goals ("break 6 stones", "beat the Chief in 30 s") make stages feel
different without new rules everywhere.

## Today
- Stage data: [StageDefinition](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Data/StageDefinition.cs) →
  [StageDef](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Stages/StageDef.cs): enemies, formation,
  stone gem count, HP/damage multipliers, map position, tutorial (D-032, D-033, D-040).
- The board is random from the seed; stones are placed by count, not by cell.
- Balance: [Tools/Balance](../../Tools/Balance/) runs a random and a greedy bot (D-026).
- The win condition is always "destroy the enemy army" ([BattleResult](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Combat/BattleResult.cs)).

## Build
1. **Logic: stage goals** (`Logic/Stages/StageGoal.cs`): kinds `DefeatArmy` (today),
   `BreakStones(count)`, `WinWithin(seconds)`; checked from the run (`StageRun`) and the
   battle result; stars still come from survivors. Tests for each kind.
2. **Logic: hand-placed cells** (optional per stage): a 6×6 layout of fixed gems and
   stones; empty cells stay random from the seed. Tests that the layout is applied
   and that the board still has a move.
3. **Data:** `goal` and `layout` fields on `StageDefinition`; old stages keep the defaults.
4. **Editor window** `Puzzle Kingdoms → Stage Editor` (`Scripts/Editor/StageEditorWindow.cs`):
   stage list, board painter (gem / stone / clear brush), goal, moves, enemies,
   "New stage" (copies the selected one), "Test difficulty" that runs the balance
   bots in the editor and shows win rate and average stars.
5. **Views:** the goal shown on the puzzle screen and on the stage card ("Break stones 2/6").
6. Move the balance runner into a shared class so the tool and `Tools/Balance` use the same code.
