# PB-33 Real music and sound

Owner: — · Milestone: M8 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-33-audio` ·
Wave: 3 (D-045) · Needs: —

## Why
The generated placeholder sounds (D-036) proved the timing; a store game needs real
music per screen and sounds that feel like swords, bows and spells.

## Today
- Every clip goes through [SoundBank](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Data/SoundBank.cs)
  (`Data/SoundBank.asset`, 15 clips with volumes) and [Sfx](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Views/Common/Sfx.cs);
  the clips are made by [PlaceholderSounds](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Editor/PlaceholderSounds.cs).
- One music loop for the whole game.

## Build
1. **Sound map:** a music track per screen (map, puzzle, battle, results) and a short
   Rally sting; list the 15 effects and what each should sound like.
2. **Source:** free commercial-use packs or a commissioned set (budget decision); every
   file into CREDITS.md first, under `Audio/`.
3. **SoundBank:** music per screen (new fields), cross-fade between screens in `Sfx`.
4. Replace the 15 clips; check volumes on a phone speaker and headphones.

## Look
Mockup: [growth screens, PB-33](https://claude.ai/artifact/PgA4zDSKLCXPzAjwZBPsLH#pb-33).

## Files
`Scripts/Data/SoundBank.cs`, `Data/SoundBank.asset`, `Views/Common/Sfx.cs`, `Audio/`, CREDITS.md.

## In Unity
Drag the new clips into the SoundBank asset's fields in the Inspector; set Load Type to
"Streaming" for music (select the clip, Inspector → Load Type).

## Not in this card
Voice, dynamic music layers.
