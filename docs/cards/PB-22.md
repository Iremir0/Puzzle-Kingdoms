# PB-22 Kingdom and buildings

Owner: — · Milestone: M7 · Size: L · Branch: `feature/PB-22-kingdom` ·
Wave: 2 (D-045) · Needs: PB-6 "go"

## Why
Gold has nothing to buy today, and the army resets after every stage, so there is
no reason to come back. Buildings that level up with gold give a small, permanent
bonus to a class: the first piece of long-term progress (GDD §10, post-MVP item 1,
"class levels with gold", shown as buildings).

## Today
- Gold and stars are saved in [PlayerProfile](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Progression/PlayerProfile.cs)
  ([ProgressionRules](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Progression/ProgressionRules.cs), save version 2).
- Unit stats come from [UnitDef](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Army/UnitDef.cs) and tier scaling.

## Build
1. **Design** (GDD §10): Barracks (Warrior HP %), Archer Tower (Archer attack speed %),
   Mage Tower (Mage damage %), Treasury (stage gold %); levels 1–10, cost curve, small
   steps (about +3 % per level). Defender / Support buildings come with PB-21.
2. **Logic:** `Kingdom` (building levels, `Upgrade` with gold check), bonuses applied when
   the army is built for a battle (one place, tested); profile save version 3 with
   migration from 2. Tests.
3. **Balance:** `Tools/Balance` gets a "kingdom level" option so stages stay winnable at
   level 0 and not trivial at the expected level.
4. **Views:** Kingdom screen (code-built like the others), bottom tab bar
   (Map · Kingdom · Commanders · Shop, items greyed until their card lands).
5. **Art:** 4–6 building models with Meshy (or simple 2D first; decide in the card).
