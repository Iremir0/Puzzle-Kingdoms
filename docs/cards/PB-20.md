# PB-20 New gems and obstacles

Owner: — · Milestone: M7 · Size: M · Branch: `feature/PB-20-obstacles` ·
Wave: 2 (D-045) · Needs: PB-6 "go"; PB-19 helps (hand-placed cells)

## Why
With one board rule set, stage 12 plays like stage 2. Obstacles split the board into
areas and change which matches are worth making; a boss that throws stones makes the
puzzle part of the boss fight.

## Today
- [BoardModel](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Logic/Puzzle/BoardModel.cs): 6×6, four gem
  types, stone gems (D-032), Blast and Prism specials (D-027, D-028) with their own
  effects in [BoardEffects](../../Assets/_Project/Scripts/Views/Puzzle/BoardEffects.cs).
- Swaps resolve all cascades at once and return steps (`MoveResult`, ARCHITECTURE).

## Build
1. **Ice:** a cell cover; a match on or next to it cracks it, the second one breaks
   it; the gem below is held until then. Tests.
2. **Chain:** the gem cannot be swapped, but it can match; a match breaks the chain. Tests.
3. **Special combos:** Blast + Prism swapped together clears every gem of one colour
   with a blast each; Blast + Blast a bigger area; Prism + Prism the whole board.
   Tests for each, plus the meter credit (recruiting stays fair).
4. **Boss board effect** on a stage: every N moves the boss drops K stones; the
   countdown is part of the session state. Tests.
5. **Views:** ice and chain drawing, combo effects, the boss banner with the countdown.
6. Stage data fields; at least two Chapter 2 stages use them (with PB-18).
